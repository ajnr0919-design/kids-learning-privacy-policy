# Privacy Policy

**App Name:** Kids Learning Games: ABC & 123  

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026  
 
**Contact Email:** ajnr0919@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

We respect the privacy of children and families.

Kids Learning Games: ABC & 123 is an educational Android application designed for preschool children. It offers activities for learning letters, numbers, animals, shapes, colors, sizes, and memory skills.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

Our app does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to play.

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on the device.

The app integrates Google AdMob for advertising. Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices, Google and its partners may automatically process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related data.

We do not directly request personal information from children.

## 3. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for young children, including children under the age of 13.

We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies.

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment with a maximum G-rated ad content setting. We do not intentionally enable personalized advertising for children.

We do not offer public chat, social networking, or user-generated content features.

## 4. Advertising

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Advertisements are intended to be age-appropriate and are configured for child-directed treatment.

The app is designed to avoid advertising during active learning activities. Advertisements may appear at appropriate transitions, subject to applicable platform policies.

Google's information about advertising and privacy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Data Storage

Learning progress and preferences may be stored locally on the user's device.

We do not operate an account-based cloud service for storing children's learning progress.

Removing the app or clearing its data may remove locally saved progress.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

Google may process information in accordance with its privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell children's personal information.

We do not intentionally share information entered by children with other users.

Third-party advertising providers may process technical and advertising-related information as described in this policy.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary data collection and protect user privacy.

No electronic system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 9. Parents and Guardians

Parents or guardians may contact us with questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's privacy practices.

Locally stored game progress can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

For information processed by third-party services, please refer to their privacy policies.
